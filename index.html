Case 01 · AI website agent / WordPress

Website sales assistant with lead qualification and human escalation

A custom WordPress AI assistant designed for service-business websites: answer questions from business context, preserve useful conversation state, collect lead details when appropriate, and route high-intent users toward a human or call path.

Built prototype

Business problem

Static pages and long forms create friction for visitors who have a question before they are ready to submit. A generic chatbot also fails if it cannot understand the service context or escalate intelligently.

What I built

A WordPress plugin-based assistant layer with LLM API integration, business-specific system prompting, conversational context, optional lead capture, and logic for human/call escalation.

Why it matters: this demonstrates a complete website → LLM → qualification → lead handoff workflow rather than a standalone prompt demo.

Case 02 · n8n / multi-stage AI operations

SEO and content automation built as a repeatable pipeline

A multi-stage n8n workflow that separates keyword intelligence, content architecture and final generation so output quality is controlled by clear roles rather than a single giant prompt.

Working workflow

Business problem

Manual research, briefing, writing, QA and publishing do not scale cleanly across many brands. A single LLM call also tends to mix strategy, writing and validation into one unreliable step.

What I built

n8n orchestration that passes structured brand context and search intent through specialized stages, producing briefs and drafts with reusable inputs and clear handoff points.

Design principle: convert one-off AI prompting into an auditable workflow with explicit stages, structured data and human-review checkpoints where quality matters.
